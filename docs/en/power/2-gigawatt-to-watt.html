What is it used for?

Useful for choosing a heater, comparing vacuum cleaners, understanding a car's power, or checking a device's consumption.

Concrete examples

  • A standard LED bulb is 10 W.
  • A vacuum cleaner is between 800 and 2000 W.
  • An electric heater is 1000 to 2000 W.
  • A car is between 90 and 150 horsepower (66 to 110 kW).

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Don't confuse watt (W) and watt-hour (Wh): the first is power, the second is energy.
  • Metric horsepower (ch) isn't the same as imperial horsepower (hp): 1 ch = 0.986 hp.
  • A 2000 W device doesn't always consume 2000 W (thermostat, eco mode).

FAQ

How many watts in 2 gigawatts?

How to convert gigawatts to watts?
Use the calculator above.

What's the difference between watt and horsepower?
The watt is the international unit of power (1 W = 1 joule per second). Horsepower (hp) is an older unit (1 hp = 745.7 W). It's still used for cars.

How many watts for a heater?
For a well-insulated room, count 100 W per m². A 15 m² bedroom needs about 1500 W. A 30 m² living room: 3000 W (often split over 2 heaters).

What power for a vacuum cleaner?
A standard vacuum is between 800 and 2000 W. Power isn't the only criterion: efficiency also depends on suction (kPa) and design.

Practical tips

  • To convert horsepower to kW: multiply by 0.735.
  • A 1000 W heater consumes 1 kWh per hour at full power.
  • To know a device's consumption: power (W) × hours used ÷ 1000 = kWh.
