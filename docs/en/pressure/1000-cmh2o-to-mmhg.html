What is it used for?

Useful for understanding weather (hPa), inflating tires (psi or bar), reading a gauge, or understanding compressor specs.

Concrete examples

  • Standard atmospheric pressure is 1013.25 hPa (or 1 atm).
  • A car tire is inflated to about 2.2 bar (32 psi).
  • A road bike tire can reach 8 bar.
  • A diver experiences 1 extra bar every 10 meters.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Don't confuse bar and psi: 1 bar ≈ 14.5 psi.
  • Atmosphere (atm) isn't exactly equal to bar (1 atm = 1.01325 bar).
  • mmHg (millimeter of mercury) is used in medicine for blood pressure, not bar.

FAQ

How many mmHg in 1000 cmH₂O?

How to convert cmH₂O to mmHg?

What's normal atmospheric pressure?
Standard atmospheric pressure is 1013.25 hPa (hectopascals), or 1013.25 mbar, or about 1 atmosphere.

At what pressure should I inflate a car tire?
Between 2 and 2.5 bars (30 to 36 psi) for a standard car. Always check the manufacturer's spec on the door jamb.

What's the difference between bar and psi?
Bar is metric (1 bar = 100,000 Pa). Psi (pound per square inch) is imperial (1 psi ≈ 0.069 bar). 1 bar ≈ 14.5 psi.

Practical tips

  • To convert bars to psi, multiply by 14.5.
  • Sea-level atmospheric pressure is about 1 bar.
  • Car tires are usually inflated between 2 and 2.5 bars.
