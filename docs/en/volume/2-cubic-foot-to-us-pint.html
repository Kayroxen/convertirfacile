What's the difference between US and UK gallons?
The US gallon is 3.785 liters. The UK gallon is 4.546 liters, about 20% more. This difference comes from British measurement history.

How many milliliters in a teaspoon?
1 teaspoon = 5 ml. One tablespoon = 15 ml. These measures are standardized in modern recipes.

How many liters in a cubic meter?
1 m³ = 1000 liters. Useful for calculating pool or tank volumes.

Practical tips
