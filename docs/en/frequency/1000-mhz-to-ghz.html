What is it used for?

Useful for understanding a processor (GHz), a screen (Hz), a radio (MHz), or a motor (RPM).

Concrete examples

  • A modern processor runs at 3 to 5 GHz.
  • A 60 Hz screen refreshes the image 60 times per second.
  • An FM station broadcasts between 87.5 and 108 MHz.
  • A car engine runs between 800 and 7000 RPM.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Don't confuse Hz and RPM: 1 Hz = 60 RPM.
  • A 120 Hz screen isn't twice as fast as a 60 Hz, but smoother.
  • Processor GHz doesn't determine everything: architecture matters too.

FAQ

How many GHz in 1000 MHz?
1000 MHz equals 1 GHz.

How to convert MHz to GHz?
Use the calculator above.

What's the difference between Hz and RPM?
Hertz (Hz) measures cycles per second. RPM measures revolutions per minute. 1 Hz = 60 RPM. A motor at 3000 RPM runs at 50 Hz.

Why do gaming screens have 144 Hz?
A 144 Hz screen displays 144 frames per second (instead of 60). It's smoother for fast games, reduces motion blur, and improves responsiveness. The gain is most visible in FPS games.

What is a processor's frequency?
It's the number of clock cycles per second (in GHz). A 3 GHz CPU performs 3 billion cycles per second. But performance also depends on core count, architecture, and cache.

Practical tips

  • To convert RPM to Hz: divide by 60.
  • To convert Hz to RPM: multiply by 60.
  • 1 kHz = 1000 Hz, 1 MHz = 1 million Hz, 1 GHz = 1 billion Hz.
