What's the difference between mass and force?
Mass (kg) is the amount of matter. Force (N) is the mechanical action (weight, thrust, pull). Weight is a force: W = m × g (with g ≈ 9.81 m/s² on Earth).

How many newtons in a kilogram-force?
1 kgf = 9.80665 newtons exactly. This value comes from Earth's gravitational acceleration. Rounded, we often use 9.81.

What is a pound-force?
The pound-force (lbf) is the Anglo-Saxon unit of force. 1 lbf = 4.44822 newtons, the weight of one pound (454 g) on Earth. It's still used in the US.

Practical tips

  • To convert kgf to N: multiply by 9.81.
  • An object's weight = mass (kg) × 9.81.
  • 1 newton ≈ 0.102 kgf (roughly the weight of an apple).
