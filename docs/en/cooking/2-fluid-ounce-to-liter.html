What is it used for?

Useful for adapting an American recipe, converting spoons to milliliters, or understanding quantities in foreign cookbooks.

Concrete examples

  • 1 tablespoon = 15 ml = 3 teaspoons.
  • 1 US cup = 240 ml = 16 tablespoons.
  • 1 standard French glass = about 200 ml.
  • 1 US fluid ounce = 29.57 ml.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • The US cup (240 ml) isn't the same as the metric cup (250 ml) or Japanese cup (200 ml).
  • A level teaspoon ≠ a heaping teaspoon.
  • Gram conversions depend on the ingredient: 1 cup of flour ≠ 1 cup of sugar.

FAQ

How many liters in 2 fluid ounces?
2 fluid ounces equals 0.0591 liters.

How to convert fluid ounces to liters?
Use the calculator above.

How many ml in a tablespoon?
1 tablespoon = 15 ml exactly. One teaspoon = 5 ml. These values are standardized in modern recipes.

What's the difference between US and UK cups?
The US cup is 240 ml. The UK cup is 250 ml (metric) or 284 ml (imperial). Use the US value for most online recipes.

How many grams in a tablespoon?
It depends on the ingredient: 1 tbsp sugar ≈ 12 g, flour ≈ 8 g, butter ≈ 14 g, water = 15 g. For liquids, 1 ml = 1 g.

Practical tips

  • Remember: 1 tbsp = 3 tsp = 15 ml.
  • For liquids, 1 ml = 1 g (except oils, syrups).
  • 1 cup flour ≈ 125 g; 1 cup sugar ≈ 200 g; 1 cup rice ≈ 185 g.
