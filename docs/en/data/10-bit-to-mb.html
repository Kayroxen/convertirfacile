Concrete examples

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Don't confuse decimal prefixes (KB, MB, GB) with binary ones (KiB, MiB, GiB).
  • A kilobyte (KB) is 1000 bytes, but a kibibyte (KiB) is 1024 bytes.
  • Drive manufacturers use decimal (1 GB = 1 billion bytes), OSes use binary.

What's the difference between bits and bytes?
1 byte = 8 bits. Internet speeds are often in bits/s, file sizes in bytes. That's why a '100 Mb/s' plan downloads at ~12.5 MB/s.

Why does my 500 GB drive show only 465 GB?
Manufacturers use 1 GB = 1,000,000,000 bytes, but Windows uses 1 GB = 1,073,741,824 bytes. Hence the display difference.

How many photos in 1 GB?
About 200 to 500 photos depending on quality. A 12 MP JPEG weighs 2 to 5 MB.

Practical tips

  • 8 bits = 1 byte. A 100 Mbit/s connection downloads at ~12.5 MB/s.
  • To estimate download time: size (MB) ÷ speed (MB/s).
  • 1 GB can hold about 250 songs or 500 photos.
