What's the difference between mass and weight?
Mass is the amount of matter (in kg). Weight is the force of gravity on that mass (in newtons). In everyday language, they're often confused.

How many grams in an ounce?
1 ounce = 28.3495 grams exactly. It's the base unit for Anglo-Saxon recipes.

Why does the UK still use stones?
The stone (14 pounds) remains the traditional unit for body weight in the UK and Ireland, even though the kilogram is official.
