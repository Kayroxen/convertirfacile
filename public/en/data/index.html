FAQ

How many data units are available on ConvertirFacile?
This category offers 14 different units and 1820 conversion pages in total, covering 182 combinations in both directions.

How to convert bits to bytes?
Use the interactive calculator on each conversion page, or click the corresponding conversion in the list above. Each page displays the result instantly.

Are data conversions free?
Yes, ConvertirFacile is a 100% free tool. No registration is required, and there is no usage limit.

Can I use these conversions on mobile?
Yes, the site is fully responsive and works perfectly on smartphone, tablet, and desktop. The calculator adapts to all screen sizes.

How accurate are the conversions?
All conversions are calculated with 4-decimal precision. For very large or very small values, scientific notation is used. Formulas are based on international standards.
