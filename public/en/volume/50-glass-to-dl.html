What is it used for?

Useful in cooking (French vs American recipes), automotive (fuel tank, consumption), or understanding quantities in imported products.

Concrete examples

  • A standard wine bottle is 75 cl, or 0.75 liters.
  • A US gallon is about 3.785 liters, a UK gallon is 4.546 liters.
  • A US cup is about 240 ml, slightly less than a French glass.
  • A barrel of oil is exactly 158.987 liters.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • US and UK gallons are different (3.78 L vs 4.55 L).
  • The US pint (473 ml) isn't the same as the UK pint (568 ml).
  • The cup isn't a universal measure — it varies by country.

FAQ

How many dl in 50 glasses?

What's the difference between US and UK gallons?
The US gallon is 3.785 liters. The UK gallon is 4.546 liters, about 20% more. This difference comes from British measurement history.

How many milliliters in a teaspoon?
1 teaspoon = 5 ml. One tablespoon = 15 ml. These measures are standardized in modern recipes.

How many liters in a cubic meter?
1 m³ = 1000 liters. Useful for calculating pool or tank volumes.

Practical tips

  • To convert liters to US gallons, multiply by 0.264.
  • One tablespoon = 3 teaspoons = 15 ml.
  • A standard US cup = 16 tablespoons = 8 fluid ounces.
